Welcome to the IT Club

The FSKTM IT Club brings students together to explore technology, share ideas and develop practical skills. Members can learn from one another and take part in activities outside the classroom.

What We Do

We organise workshops, technology events and student projects. Members can learn about programming, cybersecurity and web development while meeting other students.
